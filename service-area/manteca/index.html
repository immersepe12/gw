Hyperbaric Oxygen and Outpatient Care for Manteca Patients

Manteca-area patients have more than one place to ask about hyperbaric oxygen therapy, so it helps to know what to compare. Generations Wellness in downtown Modesto is about 20 miles south of Manteca on CA-99, roughly 21 minutes off-peak, and every course is physician-directed.

Choosing Well

What to Look for When Choosing HBOT Near Manteca

Hyperbaric oxygen therapy is also available in Manteca itself. We think patients should compare options carefully, and we encourage you to ask every center, including ours, the same questions:

  1. Does a physician assess me before I start? A physician should review your history, medications and ear and lung health, and decide whether HBOT is appropriate for you.
  2. What kind of chamber is it, and what pressure will I be treated at? The Undersea and Hyperbaric Medical Society (UHMS) defines HBOT as breathing near-100% oxygen in a chamber at 1.4 atmospheres absolute (ATA) or more, and every UHMS-recognized indication calls for 2.0 ATA or higher. Soft-sided "mild" chambers typically run at about 1.3 ATA on air, which does not meet that definition. Learn more about treatment pressure and chamber type.
  3. Which conditions do you treat, and on what evidence? Look for a center that is clear about which uses are FDA-cleared indications and which are still being researched.
  4. Will you keep my own doctors informed? Ask how your assessment and progress are shared with the clinicians who already treat you.
  5. What safety standards apply? Medical hyperbaric chambers fall under recognized codes, including NFPA 99 for the facility and ASME PVHO-1 for the pressure vessel.
  6. What will the full course cost? Ask for pricing on the whole physician-recommended course, not just one session.

For a longer checklist, read questions to ask any HBOT clinic, and see our chambers and standards.

Questions

Manteca-Area Questions

Is there a hyperbaric chamber in Manteca?

Yes, HBOT is available in the Manteca area. Generations Wellness is a physician-directed option in downtown Modesto, about 21 minutes south off-peak. Wherever you go, ask about physician assessment, treatment pressure and coordination with your doctors. A physician assessment is mandatory for every patient here.

Will Kaiser Manteca cover HBOT at your center?

We currently offer HBOT on a self-pay basis and do not bill Kaiser or other insurance. Kaiser members usually receive specialty care within Kaiser, so ask your Kaiser team first; we are happy to explain your options honestly. With your permission, we share your assessment and progress with your doctors.

How long is the drive from Tracy?

Longer than from Manteca, since most routes use I-205 and CA-120 to reach CA-99 first. Check a map app at your usual travel time, and talk with us about a consistent daily slot. The CA-120 and CA-99 interchange can get congested at commute times.

Can my Manteca Wound Center clinician stay involved?

Yes. With your permission, we share your assessment and progress with your wound care team, so HBOT fits alongside the care they provide. HBOT with us does not replace that care. For wounds, HBOT is considered only when a wound meets FDA-cleared criteria, and your physician decides whether it is appropriate for you.

What pressure should a medical hyperbaric chamber reach?

The UHMS definition of HBOT starts at 1.4 ATA, and its recognized indications use 2.0 ATA or more. Your physician prescribes the pressure for your plan. Soft-sided "mild" chambers typically run at about 1.3 ATA on air, which does not meet that definition. Our hard-shell monoplace chambers use medical-grade oxygen at a physician-prescribed pressure.
