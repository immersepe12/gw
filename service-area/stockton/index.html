Hyperbaric Oxygen Therapy for Stockton Patients

If you live in Stockton and HBOT has come up in your care, Generations Wellness in downtown Modesto is about 33 miles south on CA-99, roughly 36 minutes off-peak. Here is the drive, what a daily course takes from San Joaquin County, and how to decide whether the trip makes sense.

Physician-directed care under Dr. Rajwinder Singh Bahia, MD · Updated September 17, 2026

The Drive

Getting Here From Stockton

Stockton is the largest city in our service area, and the trip to our center is one of the simplest: a straight run south on CA-99. You pass through Manteca at about the halfway point, then continue into Modesto and head downtown to H Street.

Honest Planning

Is a Daily Course Realistic From Stockton?

It can be, but it is worth doing the math first. For the conditions HBOT is cleared for, a physician commonly recommends 20 to 40 sessions, usually one a day on weekdays. A session runs about 90 to 120 minutes including pressurization and depressurization. Add roughly 70 to 75 minutes of round-trip driving from central Stockton, plus time to check in, and many Stockton patients should budget around three to three and a half hours per treatment day.

Stockton patients who manage a full course well tend to do a few things:

  • Keep the same appointment time every day, ideally outside the heaviest CA-99 commute hours.
  • Share the driving. A spouse, adult child or friend who can take some of the trips makes a long course far easier.
  • Combine the trip with other plans in Modesto, such as errands or other appointments, so the drive does double duty.

If a daily trip to Modesto does not fit your life, that is a reasonable conclusion. Hospital-based programs also exist in the region; for example, Adventist Health Lodi Memorial operates a Wound & Hyperbaric Center in Lodi. Choose what works best for your situation. Our guide to hospital-based versus independent HBOT centers explains how the two settings differ, and what your first session involves describes the time in the chamber itself.

Stockton's Care Landscape

Your Stockton Doctors Stay Part of Your Care

Stockton has a substantial medical community. If you live there, your care may already involve a hospital such as St. Joseph's Medical Center on North California Street, Dameron Hospital on West Acacia Street or San Joaquin General Hospital in French Camp, a wound clinic such as Pacific Wound Center near Quail Lakes, or a community clinic or specialist in town. We name them only as part of the local picture, not as partners or recommendations.

Coming to Modesto for HBOT does not mean leaving those doctors behind. With your permission, we share your physician assessment and progress with the clinicians who already treat you, so hyperbaric sessions fit alongside your existing plan. If you are dealing with a wound that is slow to close, our page on non-healing wounds explains when HBOT is considered. Stockton physicians can read how we work with referring physicians.

Why Make the Trip

Why Stockton Patients Choose a Physician-Directed Center

A drive of more than half an hour each way is a real commitment, so it helps to know what you are traveling for. Hyperbaric oxygen therapy means breathing medical-grade oxygen inside a pressurized chamber, and how it is delivered matters.

  • Hard-shell monoplace chambers. Each session takes place in a single-person, rigid chamber at a pressure your physician prescribes, not a soft-sided "mild" chamber.
  • A mandatory physician assessment. No one begins a course until a physician has reviewed their history, medications and safety screening, and decided whether HBOT is appropriate.
  • Care directed by Dr. Rajwinder Singh Bahia, MD. Your treatment plan and progress reviews sit under physician direction from start to finish.

Questions

Stockton-Area Questions

Is there hyperbaric oxygen therapy near Stockton, CA?

Yes. Hospital-based programs operate in the region, such as the Wound & Hyperbaric Center at Adventist Health Lodi Memorial. Generations Wellness offers physician-directed outpatient HBOT in downtown Modesto, about 36 minutes south of Stockton off-peak via CA-99.

How long does the drive from Stockton take during rush hour?

Off-peak, it is about 36 minutes. Weekday commute traffic on CA-99 can make it longer, so check a map app at your planned departure time and allow a buffer for your first visit.

Does my Stockton health plan or Medi-Cal cover HBOT with you?

We currently offer HBOT on a self-pay basis and do not bill insurance, Medicare or Medi-Cal. Ask us and we will explain your options honestly, or read our cost guide.

Can my wound clinic in Stockton stay involved?

Yes. With your permission, we share your assessment and progress with the clinicians treating you in Stockton, so HBOT fits alongside your existing wound care.

Do I need a referral to come from Stockton?

You can call us directly, or your doctor can contact us. Either way, a physician assessment comes before any course of HBOT.
