Hyperbaric Oxygen and Outpatient Care for Turlock Patients

From Turlock, Generations Wellness in downtown Modesto is about 14 miles north on CA-99, roughly 19 minutes off-peak. That is close enough that a daily course of HBOT usually becomes a routine commute. Here is the route, a realistic daily schedule, and how your Turlock doctors stay involved.

Medically reviewed by Dr. Rajwinder Singh Bahia, MD · September 23, 2026

The Drive

Getting Here From Turlock

Turlock sits on CA-99 in the southern part of Stanislaus County, which makes the trip to downtown Modesto one of the shortest in our service area. Head north on CA-99, pass Ceres, and follow the downtown exits in Modesto to H Street.

Your Weekly Rhythm

A Daily Course From Turlock Is Usually Routine

For the conditions HBOT is cleared for, a physician commonly recommends 20 to 40 sessions, typically one a day, five days a week. Each session takes about 90 to 120 minutes including pressurization and depressurization. With a round trip of under 40 minutes off-peak, most Turlock patients can plan on about two and a half to three hours for a treatment day, door to door.

That makes HBOT easier to fit around ordinary life than it is for patients coming from farther away. A few habits help:

  • Book a consistent time. The same slot each day turns the drive into a habit and makes it easier to arrange rides or time off work.
  • Think about when you drive. If you commute or work shifts, a session outside the busiest CA-99 hours can keep the trip close to 19 minutes.
  • Plan for how you'll feel. Many people feel fine to drive after a session, but some feel tired. If that is you, a family member or friend can take the wheel.

Turlock's Care Landscape

Working Alongside Your Turlock Doctors

Many Turlock-area residents receive hospital care at Emanuel Medical Center, and cancer care at the Emanuel Cancer Center. We mention them because they are part of the local health care picture, not because of any partnership or endorsement.

If your doctors in Turlock have raised the idea of HBOT, you don't need to change who treats you. With your permission, we share your physician assessment and progress with them, so hyperbaric sessions fit alongside the care you already receive. Two of the FDA-cleared indications that people often ask about are delayed radiation injury after cancer treatment and certain diabetic foot ulcers. In every case, a physician determines whether HBOT is appropriate for you. Physicians can read how we work with referring physicians.

Worth the Trip North

What Turlock Patients Get at a Physician-Directed Center

Not every hyperbaric experience is the same. Medical hyperbaric oxygen therapy means breathing medical-grade oxygen inside a pressurized chamber under physician direction. Here is what that looks like at our Modesto center:

  • Rigid, hard-shell monoplace chambers. You have the chamber to yourself, and it is pressurized to the level your physician prescribes.
  • A physician assessment before anything else. Your medical history, medications and safety screening are reviewed first, and you get a straight answer about whether HBOT fits.
  • Physician direction throughout. Care at Generations Wellness is directed by Dr. Rajwinder Singh Bahia, MD, from your first assessment to your final session.

More Than HBOT

Other Care for Turlock Families at Our Modesto Practice

Generations Wellness is a physician-led outpatient practice with nine services under one roof, not only a hyperbaric center. At roughly 19 minutes each way, Turlock is close enough for services that need two or three visits a week, such as physical therapy after a knee or shoulder injury, or weekly pediatric speech therapy after school.

Services Turlock patients ask about

  • Physical therapy for back, knee, neck and shoulder pain, sports injuries and rehab after surgery. Under California law, as of 2026, you can start physical therapy without a referral for a limited time; see physical therapy without a referral.
  • Pediatric speech therapy and pediatric physical therapy for late talking, speech sounds, stuttering and motor milestones.
  • Occupational therapy for children and adults, including hand therapy and sensory processing.
  • Wound care for wounds that are slow to heal, with hyperbaric oxygen therapy considered only when a wound meets FDA-cleared criteria.
  • Pain management led by a physician, with a non-opioid-first approach, and counseling for anxiety, depression, grief and coping with illness.
  • IV therapy ordered by a physician after a health screening.

Free and school-based options for Turlock children

Private therapy is not the only route. For children under 3 in Stanislaus County, California Early Start evaluations are arranged through Valley Mountain Regional Center. From age 3, Turlock Unified School District or your local district is responsible for evaluating children who may need special education, including speech and language services, through an IEP. Many families use school services and private therapy together. Our guide to private vs. school speech therapy explains how they differ.

Questions

Turlock-Area Questions

Is there hyperbaric oxygen therapy in Turlock?

Generations Wellness does not have a Turlock location. Our physician-directed center is in downtown Modesto, about 19 minutes north of Turlock off-peak via CA-99. The drive is about 14 miles, so a daily course of HBOT usually becomes a routine commute. Use street parking or a nearby lot, then look for Suite C-1 at 1801 H Street. Booking a consistent time each day can make the routine easier.

Can my doctor at Emanuel Medical Center or the Emanuel Cancer Center refer me?

Any of your doctors can contact us about you, or you can call us yourself. With your permission, we keep your Turlock doctors updated on your assessment and progress. HBOT complements the care you already get, so your Turlock doctors stay involved. If you are reaching out on your own, our physician assessment is the first step to see whether HBOT is right for you.

Will my Turlock employer's insurance or Medi-Cal pay for HBOT with you?

We currently offer HBOT on a self-pay basis and do not bill insurance, Medicare or Medi-Cal. Ask us and we will explain your options honestly. We review pricing with you clearly before you commit to a course, so there are no surprises. Your physician assessment also helps confirm whether HBOT is appropriate for your condition before any sessions begin.

What about Ceres, Hughson, Delhi or Livingston?

You are welcome here too, and the same directions apply. Ceres is closer to Modesto than Turlock is; Delhi and Livingston are a little farther south. From most of these towns, CA-99 north is the simplest route into downtown Modesto. Look for Suite C-1 at 1801 H Street. Our team can help you pick a session time that fits your drive.

Can I drive myself home after a session?

Many people do, but some feel tired afterward. Ask your physician at your assessment, and bring a driver for your first session if you are unsure. The drive back to Turlock is about 19 minutes off-peak on CA-99. Plan a little extra time after early sessions until you know how you feel. Your physician can review any individual restrictions with you.
