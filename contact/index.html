Beyond Modesto

Areas We Serve

Our center on H Street in downtown Modesto welcomes patients from across Stanislaus County and the Central Valley — including Turlock, Ceres, Manteca, Ripon, Riverbank, Oakdale, and the Stockton area. Most of our neighbors reach us within a 15–40 minute drive via Highway 99. If you're planning a full course of sessions from out of town, mention it when you call — we'll help you build a schedule that makes the drive worthwhile.

No Surprises

What Happens After You Reach Out

  1. A brief phone conversation

    We listen to what's going on, answer first questions, and set up your assessment. See what to expect at a session beforehand if you're curious.

  2. Your physician assessment

    A physician reviews your history and condition, and gives you an honest answer about whether HBOT is appropriate for you.

  3. A personalized plan — if it's right

    If HBOT fits your care plan, you'll get a clear session schedule, clear pricing, and clear expectations. If it doesn't, we'll tell you that too.
