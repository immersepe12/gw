Our Services

Nine Outpatient Services, One Care Team

Every service is outpatient: you visit, receive care and go home the same day. Your care team shares one plan, so your providers know what the others are doing.

  • Physical Therapy

    One-on-one outpatient physical therapy for pain, injuries, surgery recovery, balance and mobility.

  • Pain Management

    A physician-led plan for back, neck, joint and nerve pain that combines medical care with therapy.

  • Counseling

    Talk therapy with licensed clinicians for adults, couples and families, in person or by video.

  • Pediatric Speech Therapy

    Speech, language and feeding support for children, with parents involved at every step.

  • Pediatric Therapy

    Pediatric physical and occupational therapy for motor skills, development and sensory needs.

  • Occupational Therapy

    Help getting back to the everyday tasks that matter, from hand and arm recovery to daily living skills.

  • Wound Care

    Assessment and ongoing care for wounds that are slow to heal, coordinated with your other doctors and, when appropriate, with HBOT.

  • IV Therapy

    Physician-ordered vitamin and hydration infusions, given in our clinic after a health screening.

  • Hyperbaric Oxygen Therapy

    Medical-grade HBOT in hard-shell monoplace chambers for FDA-cleared indications such as selected non-healing wounds and delayed radiation injury.

Featured Service

Advanced Oxygen-Based Therapy for Healing and Recovery

Hyperbaric oxygen therapy helps deliver higher levels of oxygen to the body's tissues in a pressurized chamber. That increased oxygen availability may support the body's natural healing response — which is why HBOT is commonly used as part of a physician-directed care plan.

What Is HBOT?

What Is Hyperbaric Oxygen Therapy?

Inside our hard-shell monoplace chambers, you breathe medical-grade oxygen under increased pressure prescribed by your physician. Under that pressure, extra oxygen dissolves directly into your blood plasma — reaching tissues where circulation or healing may be compromised.

Sessions are calm and restful: most patients simply relax while the chamber does the work. Your course is planned, supervised, and adjusted under the guidance of Dr. Rajwinder Singh Bahia, MD, from the very first assessment.

Getting Started

Getting Started Is Simple

Whichever service brings you in, the first steps are the same.

  1. Reach out

    Call (369) 222-0979 or (209) 809-0022. We'll ask what brings you in, answer first questions and book your first visit.

  2. Your assessment

    A licensed clinician reviews your history, goals and current treatment, and tells you honestly whether the service is right for you.

  3. Your personalized plan

    You get a written plan: what will happen, how often you'll come in, what it costs, and how we'll measure progress.

Important

An assessment comes before every treatment plan. If you are having a medical emergency, call 911. If you are in crisis, call or text 988.
