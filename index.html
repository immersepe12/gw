Physician-Directed Care

The Therapy

Advanced Oxygen-Based Therapy for Healing and Recovery

Hyperbaric oxygen therapy helps deliver higher levels of oxygen to the body's tissues in a pressurized chamber. That increased oxygen availability may support the body's natural healing response — which is why HBOT is commonly used as part of a physician-directed care plan.

What Is HBOT?

What Is Hyperbaric Oxygen Therapy?

Inside a hyperbaric chamber, you breathe a higher concentration of oxygen under gently increased pressure. Under that pressure, extra oxygen dissolves directly into your blood plasma — reaching tissues where circulation or healing may be compromised.

Sessions are calm and restful: most patients simply relax while the chamber does the work. Your course is planned, supervised, and adjusted by a physician from the very first assessment.

Why Oxygen Matters

How HBOT May Support Your Healing

Every benefit below reflects the same mechanism — more oxygen, delivered where your body needs it most.

Wound Healing and Tissue Recovery

Supports wound healing and tissue recovery by improving the oxygen supply that healing tissue depends on — session after session.

Oxygen Delivery to Compromised Tissues

Helps improve oxygen delivery to tissues where circulation is impaired — the areas that struggle most to heal on their own.

Recovery After Radiation-Related Tissue Injury

May assist healing after radiation-related tissue injury, supporting tissue that needs long-term help rebuilding its blood supply.
