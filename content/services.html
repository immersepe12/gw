How We Work

One Practice, One Shared Care Plan

Many health problems don't fit neatly into one specialty. A slow-healing wound may also mean pain, limited walking and worry. A child with a speech delay may also have trouble with fine motor skills. Chronic back pain often touches sleep, mood and work. When each of those needs is handled at a different office, families end up repeating their story and carrying information between providers.

Generations Wellness Medical Practice brings those services together. We are a physician-led outpatient practice, directed by Dr. Rajwinder Singh Bahia, MD, an internal medicine physician. Patients come in for visits, get care and go home; we do not provide inpatient or emergency care.

Here is what that means for you:

  • An assessment first. A licensed clinician evaluates you or your child before any treatment plan begins.
  • One shared care plan. If you use more than one service, your clinicians work from the same plan and talk to each other directly.
  • A physician in the building. Therapists can bring medical questions to our physician, which can make steps like signing a therapy plan of care simpler.
  • Coordination with your own doctors, with your permission, so our care fits alongside theirs.
  • Honest answers, including when another service or setting would serve you better.

Not Sure Where to Start?

How to Choose the Right Service

A starting point only. A clinician helps confirm the right service after an assessment.
If your main concern is…Start with
A wound that hasn't healed in a few weeksWound care
Pain or stiffness that limits movement, walking or sportPhysical therapy
Ongoing pain that affects sleep, work or moodPain management
Trouble with hand tasks, self-care or sensory needsOccupational therapy
A baby or child slow to sit, crawl or walkPediatric physical therapy
A child who is late to talk or hard to understandPediatric speech therapy
Stress, anxiety, low mood or griefCounseling
A physician-confirmed FDA-cleared HBOT indicationHyperbaric oxygen therapy

Still unsure? Call us and describe what's going on. Our team will suggest a starting point, and a clinician can refer you across services once you're seen. You don't need to work out the medical labels yourself.

When to go somewhere else first

Some problems need emergency care, not an outpatient visit. Call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room for chest pain, trouble breathing, signs of stroke, a wound with spreading redness and fever, sudden weakness, loss of bladder or bowel control, or thoughts of harming yourself. For a mental health crisis, call or text 988.

Questions

Our Services: Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use more than one service at your practice?

Yes. Many patients do, and your clinicians share one care plan so services work together instead of separately.

Do I need a referral?

It depends on the service and your plan. Under California law, as of 2026, physical therapy can start without a referral for a limited time. Call us and we'll explain what applies.

Do you treat emergencies?

No. We are an outpatient practice. For emergencies call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room; for a mental health crisis call or text 988.

Who leads care at Generations Wellness?

Our practice is physician-led, directed by Dr. Rajwinder Singh Bahia, MD, an internal medicine physician, working with licensed therapists and counselors.

How much do your services cost?

Costs depend on the service and your plan. Call us to ask about current pricing and payment options; we review costs with you before treatment.
